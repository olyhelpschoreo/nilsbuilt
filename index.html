Common questions

How long does it take?

Most one-page sites are ready in a few days, with previews along the way.

What do I need to give you?

Just the basics — what your business does, any logo or photos you have, and how people reach you.

Can I change it later?

Yes — small updates are easy. Just send me what you'd like changed.

Do I pay upfront?

No. You see a preview first and only pay once you're happy with it.

Do I own the website?

Yes — the design, the words and the pictures are yours to keep. If you ever want to move it elsewhere, you can take it with you.

Is there a monthly fee?

Not from me. Your site is hosted free, so once it's paid for it simply stays online. A personal address like yourbusiness.se is optional and costs a small yearly fee paid straight to the domain provider.
